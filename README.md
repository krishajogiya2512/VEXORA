# VEXORA
### AI-Powered Social Media Identity Intelligence & Threat Detection

**VEXORA** is an AI-powered cybersecurity platform designed to identify potentially fake social media accounts, analyze suspicious online behavior, and streamline reporting for investigation by authorized agencies and social media platforms.

Developed in response to the ITBP problem statement, VEXORA aims to strengthen digital identity security through intelligent detection, evidence-based analysis, and centralized incident reporting.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Fake social media accounts can be used for impersonation, fraudulent communication, misinformation, and other malicious activities. Identifying suspicious profiles across platforms and coordinating timely reporting can be challenging.

VEXORA aims to address this challenge through an intelligent platform that helps detect suspicious accounts, organize supporting evidence, and facilitate structured reporting to relevant authorities and social media platforms.

## ✨ Key Features

- **AI-Powered Detection:** Analyze profile characteristics and behavioral patterns to identify potentially fake accounts.
- **Profile Risk Assessment:** Evaluate suspicious indicators and generate risk scores to support further review.
- **Multi-Platform Intelligence:** Designed to support the analysis of accounts across social media platforms.
- **Evidence Management:** Organize profile details, suspicious indicators, and investigation records.
- **Centralized Reporting:** Facilitate structured reporting and case tracking for authorized personnel.
- **Investigation Dashboard:** Present findings and risk indicators in an accessible interface.
- **Privacy-Aware Design:** Support responsible analysis and protect sensitive information.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Profile Input:** Submit a publicly accessible profile or permitted account data for analysis.
2. **Feature Analysis:** Examine available profile information, activity patterns, and other relevant indicators.
3. **Risk Evaluation:** Apply detection rules or machine learning models to identify suspicious characteristics.
4. **Evidence Generation:** Present relevant indicators and an explainable risk assessment.
5. **Human Review:** Allow authorized reviewers to assess findings and minimize false positives.
6. **Report & Escalate:** Facilitate reports to the appropriate platform or authorized agency for further action.

## 🛡️ Expected Impact

- Improve the identification of potentially fake social media accounts.
- Support faster, evidence-based cybersecurity investigations.
- Reduce the manual effort involved in organizing suspicious-account reports.
- Enable structured coordination between authorized agencies and social media platforms.
- Contribute to a safer and more trustworthy digital environment.

## ⚖️ Responsible Use

VEXORA is intended to support cybersecurity analysis and legitimate investigations. A suspiciousness score is an indicator for review, not proof that an account is fake or that its owner has committed wrongdoing.

Account suspension, identity disclosure, and legal action must follow applicable laws, platform policies, and authorized procedures. The system should use permitted data sources and appropriate privacy safeguards.
